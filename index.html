What is JG Nutrition Magnesium Bisglycinate Liquid Drops?

It is a raspberry-flavored magnesium bisglycinate supplement presented in a 2 fl oz liquid dropper bottle. The final description and Supplement Facts panel must be checked against the manufacturer's confirmed label.

How much should I take?

Dosage instructions have not been provided for this design concept. Follow the approved product label and consult a qualified healthcare professional if you have questions about appropriate supplementation.

Can I take it with other medications or supplements?

Magnesium supplements can interact with certain medications. Consult your healthcare professional or pharmacist about your personal situation and any timing requirements before use.

Does liquid magnesium absorb better than gummies or capsules?

Absorption depends on factors such as the magnesium compound, formulation, dose, and individual circumstances. The liquid format alone does not establish superior absorption; any comparative claim requires reliable evidence.

Can I buy a subscription?

The prototype demonstrates a subscription selector, but recurring delivery, discounts, billing, cancellation terms, and Shopify subscription integration would need client approval before launch.

What is your refund policy?

The client's official return and refund policy has not yet been provided. The published page must display the actual policy and its terms, rather than an invented guarantee.

Is this a live Shopify product page?

No. This is a standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript design and interaction prototype. Live inventory, pricing, payments, shipping, Meta Pixel, customer reviews, and checkout require implementation and verification in the client's Shopify store.
